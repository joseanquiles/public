1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Math Trainer handles information when you use the application. Math Trainer is an educational app designed to help users practice basic math operations and improve mental calculation skills.

2. Information We Collect

Math Trainer does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information from users.

The app may store basic progress or training information locally on your device, such as scores, completed exercises, settings, or training history. This information remains on your device and is not sent to the developer or to any external server.

3. How We Use Information

Any information stored locally by the app is used only to provide the app functionality, such as displaying progress, saving preferences, or improving the user experience inside the app.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share user data with third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

Math Trainer does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services.

6. Children’s Privacy

Math Trainer may be used by children as an educational tool. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that any personal information has been provided through the app, please contact us so we can address the issue.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Math Trainer does not collect personal information or store data on external servers, there is no personal data retained by the developer. Any local app data can be deleted by uninstalling the app or clearing the app data from the device settings.

8. Security

We take privacy seriously. Since the app does not transmit personal data to external servers, privacy risks are minimized.
