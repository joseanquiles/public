Política de privacidad de Sky & Earth

Última actualización: 11 de octubre de 2026

En resumen: Sky & Earth no tiene cuentas de usuario, ni publicidad, ni analítica, ni seguimiento, y su desarrollador no tiene servidores propios ni recibe ningún dato tuyo. Para mostrarte lo que hay a tu alrededor, la app envía tu ubicación aproximada a algunos servicios públicos de datos. La imagen de la cámara nunca sale de tu dispositivo, salvo que tú mismo compartas una foto.

Quién es el responsable

Sky & Earth (en español, «Cielo y Tierra») es una app desarrollada por José Antonio Quiles Follana. Para cualquier consulta sobre privacidad puedes escribir a joseanquiles@gmail.com.

Qué datos usa la app y para qué

DatoPara qué se usaDónde se trata
Ubicación (con tu permiso)Calcular dónde están el Sol, la Luna, los planetas y las estrellas desde tu posición, y qué ciudades, montañas, lugares y aviones tienes alrededor.Los cálculos se hacen en tu dispositivo. Solo se envía una ubicación aproximada a los servicios indicados más abajo.

Preferencias (idioma, capas que se muestran)Recordar tus ajustes.Solo en tu dispositivo.

Para mostrar información actualizada, la app consulta estos servicios públicos. Como en cualquier conexión a internet, cada servicio recibe la dirección IP de tu dispositivo y lo que la app le pide; la app no les envía ningún identificador tuyo ni ningún otro dato personal.

Lo que la app no hace

  • No tiene registro ni cuentas de usuario.
  • No muestra publicidad.
  • No usa herramientas de analítica ni de seguimiento, y no te rastrea entre apps o sitios web.
  • No vende ni comparte datos personales.
  • No guarda ni envía fotos ni vídeos de la cámara por su cuenta: solo las fotos que tú haces, y solo donde tú decides.
  • El desarrollador no recibe ni almacena ningún dato tuyo.

Tus derechos y cómo controlar tus datos

Puedes conceder o retirar en cualquier momento los permisos de ubicación, cámara, fotos y notificaciones en Ajustes › Privacidad y seguridad de tu dispositivo. Sin ubicación, la app no puede situar los objetos; sin cámara, las vistas de realidad aumentada se muestran sin imagen de fondo.

Como el desarrollador no guarda ningún dato tuyo, no hay nada que consultar, rectificar ni borrar en sus sistemas. Al desinstalar la app se eliminan del dispositivo tus preferencias y los datos en caché. De acuerdo con el Reglamento General de Protección de Datos (RGPD), puedes escribir al correo indicado arriba para cualquier consulta, y tienes derecho a presentar una reclamación ante la Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Menores

La app no está dirigida específicamente a menores y no recoge datos personales de nadie, tampoco de menores.

Cambios en esta política

Si la app cambia la forma en que usa los datos, esta página se actualizará y se cambiará la fecha de la parte superior.

Sky & Earth privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

In short: Sky & Earth has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and its developer runs no servers of their own and receives none of your data. To show you what is around you, the app sends your approximate location to some public data services. The camera image never leaves your device unless you share a photo yourself.

Who is responsible

Sky & Earth is an app developed by José Antonio Quiles Follana. For any privacy question you can write to joseanquiles@gmail.com.

What data the app uses and why

DataWhat it is used forWhere it is processed
Location (with your permission)Working out where the Sun, the Moon, the planets and the stars are from where you stand, and which cities, mountains, places and aircraft are around you.Calculations are done on your device. Only an approximate location is sent to the services listed below.
Camera (with your permission)Showing the background image in the augmented reality views.On your device only. The image is not recorded or sent anywhere. A photo is only taken when you tap the camera button.
Photos (with your permission, add only)Saving to your library the photos you take with the names drawn on them.On your device only. The app cannot see or read your library. If you share a photo, you send it yourself, to whoever you choose.
Notifications (with your permission)Alerting you to the sky events you choose in Settings (ISS passes, full moon, meteor showers, eclipses, comets and exceptional events).Calculated and scheduled on your device from your last location. Nothing is sent to any server.
Motion sensors (compass, gyroscope, accelerometer)Knowing where you are pointing the device.On your device only.
Preferences (language, layers shown)Remembering your settings.On your device only.
Downloaded data (satellite orbits, terrain elevation)Working faster and offline.Kept in the app's cache, on your device.

External services the app connects to

To show up-to-date information, the app queries these public services. As with any internet connection, each service receives your device's IP address and what the app asks for; the app sends them no identifier of yours and no other personal data.

ServiceWhat it receivesWhat for
Apple Maps (MapKit)Your location, to search for places nearby, and the position of the place you look up.Places of interest, stations, airports and services, and the street-level view of a place. «Directions» opens Apple's Maps app with the destination; from there Apple's policy applies.
adsb.lolYour location rounded to about 1 km.Aircraft flying around you.
adsbdbA flight's callsign (e.g. "IBE3456") and, when you open its sheet, the aircraft's code, without your location.The flight's origin and destination, and the aircraft's model and operator.
OpenStreetMap (Overpass API)Your location rounded to about 100 m.Viewpoints, health centres, mountain huts, caves, drinking water and rivers.
Amazon Web Services (Terrain Tiles)The map areas around you (several kilometres each).Terrain elevation.
CelesTrakNo location data.Satellite orbits.
Minor Planet Center (IAU)No location data.Orbits of comets and of bright and near-Earth asteroids.
Open-MeteoYour approximate location, rounded to about 1 km, when you open «Sky» or «Sky events».Cloud forecast for tonight.
Wikipedia and Wikimedia CommonsThe name of the object you look up (e.g. "Mars") or of a photo, without your location.A summary and picture of the object, and photos of places.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. Without an internet connection the app keeps working, without the data from these services.

What the app does not do

  • It has no sign-up and no user accounts.
  • It shows no advertising.
  • It uses no analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not sell or share personal data.
  • It does not save or send photos or video from the camera on its own: only the photos you take, and only where you decide.
  • The developer receives and stores none of your data.

Your rights and how to control your data

You can grant or withdraw the location, camera, photos and notifications permissions at any time in your device's Settings › Privacy & Security. Without location the app cannot place the objects; without the camera the augmented reality views are shown without a background image.

As the developer keeps none of your data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete in their systems. Uninstalling the app removes your preferences and cached data from the device. Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you may write to the address above with any question, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes the way it uses data, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
