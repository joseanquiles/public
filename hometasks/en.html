Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short: HomeTasks does not collect, send or share any data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Who we are

HomeTasks is an app for keeping track of household chores with points, developed by José Antonio Quiles Follana (“we”). This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the app.

Information stored in the app

To work, the app stores on your device the information you enter yourself:

  • people at home: name, nickname and an avatar or photo;
  • tasks, penalties and rewards you create, with their points and pictures;
  • the records of who did what and when, with an optional note;
  • preferences, such as the app language.

This information is saved only inside the app on your device. The app does not connect to the internet and never sends it to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

Photos

If you choose a photo for a person, task or penalty, you pick it with the system photo picker. The app only receives the photo you choose; it does not have access to the rest of your photo library and does not ask for permission. The photo is resized and saved inside the app.

Exports and backups

You can export a PDF report, a CSV file with the records or a JSON backup with all the data (including photos). This only happens when you ask for it, and the file is shared through the system share sheet to the place you choose (for example Files, Mail or Messages). From then on, that file is handled by the app or service you chose. Keep backups in a safe place, because they contain all your data.

Like any other app, the HomeTasks data can be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), which are managed by Apple according to your settings.

No tracking and no third parties

HomeTasks does not use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or any third-party service, and does not track you across apps or websites. In the App Store it is labelled “Data Not Collected”.

Children

HomeTasks is designed for families, and children may appear in it as people at home. Because the app does not collect any data, no information about children is collected or shared. We recommend that an adult sets up the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete people, tasks and penalties in the app, and delete records between two dates in Settings → Delete records. Deleting the app removes all its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
